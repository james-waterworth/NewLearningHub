Educator Toolkit

Planning to deliver a Learning Half Day session? To help staff find and book onto it, your session must be added to the Electronic Staff Record (ESR) before promotion and delivery. This guide explains what to submit, how to submit it, and the timescales involved.

Facilitator support

Follow these steps to link your training session to the Learning Hub and make sure it is recorded for learners.

1

Request the form

Request the form from your Backbone or Care Group Learning Half Day representative.

2

Complete and return

Complete the form and email it to your Learning Half Day representative.

3

Session published

Your session is created on ESR, displayed on the Learning Hub, and added to the calendar.

How to complete your class submission

Expand each field for guidance and examples. Providing complete details helps us add your class to the programme without needing to request more information.

Enter the broad subject your class sits under. This acts as the overall group name in the programme, so keep it short and general; one or two words is usually enough.

Examples: Poverty Proofing, Autism.

Don’t include the audience, level, or date here; those details belong in the class title.

Enter the full title exactly as it should appear in the Learning Half Day programme. Make it clear and specific, including the audience or level where relevant. If you run multiple classes under one course topic, give each class a different title.

Examples: Poverty Proofing: Children’s Care Group only; Autism: A beginner’s guide to autism.

Enter the Learning Half Day date when your class will run. Classes can only take place on published dates, so check the current schedule first.

A date that does not match the schedule may be returned for correction and delay publication.

Enter the start time using the 24-hour clock, for example 09:30 or 13:15. The complete class must fit within a published Learning Half Day session time.

Classes outside these times may need to be rearranged before they can be added.

Provide the total duration and the start and finish times, for example “2 hours, 10:00–12:00” or “90 minutes, 13:30–15:00”. This helps learners understand the commitment and confirms the class fits within the half day.

Learning Representatives support facilitators with submissions, review forms, follow up missing information, and connect facilitators with the Learning Half Day team and ESR. If you are unsure who your representative is, contact your Care Group’s Learning Half Day lead.

Important submission guidance

  • Course availability: If the course is already on ESR, mark “Yes” in Column 3 and complete only the class delivery columns. If it is not on ESR, complete all blue columns so the course can be set up.
  • Submission: Email the completed form to your Backbone or Care Group Learning Half Day representative.
  • After the session: Complete the orange columns to record attendance and DNAs.
